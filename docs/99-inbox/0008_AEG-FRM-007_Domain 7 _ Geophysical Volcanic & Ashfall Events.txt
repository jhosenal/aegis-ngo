### 1. Executive Summary

Domain 7 governs sudden-onset geophysical volcanic eruptions, explosive ashfall dispersion, and secondary lahar/pyroclastic hazards. Unlike meteorological storms or seismic ground shaking, volcanic events present unique structural collapse risks from heavy ash accumulation on roofs, severe respiratory hazards from fine silicate glass particulates, and broad agricultural contamination.

This specification defines the exact deterministic mathematical formulas, data normalization rules from **Global Volcanism Program (Smithsonian / GVP)** and **VAAC (Volcanic Ash Advisory Centers)**, Sphere Project survival shortfalls, and deterministic Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) state gates executed by the Aegis NGO engine.

### 3. Core Mathematical Formulations

#### 3.1 Roof Ash Load ($M_{\text{load}}$)
Structural roof collapse risk scales directly with the weight of accumulated wet or dry ash per square meter:

$$M_{\text{load}} = h_{\text{ash}} \cdot \left(\frac{\rho_{\text{ash}}}{1000.0}\right) \quad [\text{kg/m}^2]$$

*Engineering Threshold:* Flat residential roofs risk structural failure when $M_{\text{load}} \ge 100 \, \text{kg/m}^2$ (equivalent to approx. $100\,\text{mm}$ of dry ash or $50\,\text{mm}$ of wet rain-soaked ash).

---

#### 3.2 Plume Dispersion & Respiratory Hazard Footprint ($R_{\text{plume}}$)
The downwind hazard radius affected by hazardous $PM_{10}$ and $PM_{2.5}$ volcanic glass aerosols:

$$R_{\text{plume}}(VEI, v_{\text{wind}}) = 15.0 \cdot (VEI + 1.0) \cdot \left(1.0 + 0.02 \cdot v_{\text{wind}}\right) \quad [\text{Kilometers}]$$

---

#### 3.3 Affected Population Exposure ($P_{\text{aff}}$)
$$\text{If } D_{\text{vent}} \le R_{\text{plume}}, \quad P_{\text{aff}} = P_0 \cdot \left(1.0 - \frac{D_{\text{vent}}}{R_{\text{plume}} + 1}\right) \quad \text{Else } P_{\text{aff}} = 0$$

---

#### 3.4 Sphere Standard Emergency Shortfalls ($\Delta R$)

1. **Heavy-Duty Particulate Masks (N95 / P100 Silicate Rated):**
   $$\Delta R_{\text{masks}} = P_{\text{aff}} \cdot 4.0 \quad [\text{Mask Units}]$$
2. **Emergency Eye Saline Wash & Protective Goggles:**
   $$\Delta R_{\text{eyewash}} = \left\lceil P_{\text{aff}} \cdot 0.2 \right\rceil \quad [\text{Units}]$$
3. **Roof Shoveling & Structural Clearing Kits:**
   $$\Delta R_{\text{shovels}} = \left\lceil \frac{P_{\text{aff}}}{25.0} \right\rceil \quad [\text{Heavy-Duty Shoveling \& PPE Kits}]$$
4. **Potable Water Reservoir Sealing & Tank Covers:**
   $$\Delta R_{\text{tank\_covers}} = \left\lceil \frac{P_{\text{aff}}}{10.0} \right\rceil \quad [\text{WASH Sealing Kits}]$$
