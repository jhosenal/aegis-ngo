### 1. Executive Summary

Domain 3 governs meteorological hazard events, focusing on extreme wind dynamics, atmospheric pressure deficits, tropical cyclone/typhoon tracks, and secondary severe weather impacts (such as localized wind damage and storm surge pressure dynamics).

This specification defines the exact deterministic mathematical formulas, data normalization rules from **NOAA / GDACS Meteorological Feeds**, Sphere Project humanitarian standards, and deterministic Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) state gates executed by the Aegis NGO engine.

### 3. Core Mathematical Formulations

#### 3.1 Dynamic Wind Pressure Load ($q_{\text{wind}}$)
Wind destruction scales quadratically with velocity ($v^2$). The kinetic pressure load exerted on temporary structures and roofs is calculated as:

$$q_{\text{wind}} = \frac{1}{2} \cdot \rho_{\text{air}} \cdot v_{\text{gust}}^2 \quad [\text{Pascals (Pa)}]$$

*(Where standard air density $\rho_{\text{air}} = 1.225 \, \text{kg/m}^3$ at standard temperature and pressure).*

---

#### 3.2 Saffir-Simpson Equivalent Category Index ($CAT_{\text{met}}$)
Determined deterministically from maximum sustained wind speed $v_{\text{wind}}$ in knots ($1\,\text{m/s} \approx 1.94384\,\text{kts}$):

$$v_{\text{kts}} = v_{\text{wind}} \cdot 1.94384$$

$$\text{Category } = \begin{cases} 0 & v_{\text{kts}} < 64 \quad (\text{Tropical Storm / Sub-cyclone}) \\ 1 & 64 \le v_{\text{kts}} < 83 \\ 2 & 83 \le v_{\text{kts}} < 96 \\ 3 & 96 \le v_{\text{kts}} < 113 \\ 4 & 113 \le v_{\text{kts}} < 137 \\ 5 & v_{\text{kts}} \ge 137 \quad (\text{Super Typhoon / Cat 5 Cyclone}) \end{cases}$$

---

#### 3.3 Severe Damage Radius Footprint ($R_{\text{damage}}$)
$$R_{\text{damage}} = R_{\text{max}} \cdot \left(1.0 + 0.15 \cdot CAT_{\text{met}}\right) \quad [\text{Kilometers}]$$

---

#### 3.4 Sphere Standard Emergency Shortfalls ($\Delta R$)

1. **Reinforced Tarpaulin & Roof Tie-Down Kits ($\Delta R_{\text{tarp}}$):**
   *(Sphere Standard: Minimum 2 heavy-duty tarpaulins per displaced household of 5)*
   $$\Delta R_{\text{tarp}} = \left\lceil \frac{P_0}{5.0} \right\rceil \cdot 2 \quad [\text{Tarpaulin Units}]$$

2. **High-Calorie Ready-to-Eat (RTE) Rations ($\Delta R_{\text{food}}$):**
   *(Sphere Standard: $2,100\,\text{kcal}$ per person per day for 7 days post-impact)*
   $$\Delta R_{\text{food}} = P_0 \cdot 7 \quad [\text{Daily RTE Ration Sets}]$$

3. **Emergency Solar Radio & Multi-Band Weather Transceivers ($\Delta R_{\text{comm}}$):**
   $$\Delta R_{\text{comm}} = \left\lceil \frac{P_0}{50.0} \right\rceil \quad [\text{Comm Units}]$$

4. **Coastal Storm Surge Evacuation Kits ($\Delta R_{\text{surge}}$):**
   $$\text{If } S_{\text{surge}} \ge 2.0\text{m}, \quad \Delta R_{\text{surge}} = \left\lceil P_0 \cdot 0.35 \right\rceil \quad \text{Else } \Delta R_{\text{surge}} = 0$$
