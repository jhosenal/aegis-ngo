## Vision

Make humanitarian coordination accessible, understandable and accountable through a shared evidence workspace.

## Mission

Help NGO teams turn disaster information and their own resource records into traceable operational assessments. OAG governs workflows, calculations and permissions; bounded language assistance helps people interact with the engine.

## Objectives and measures

1. A repeatable incident-to-resource-assessment workflow, with source, as-of time and assumptions on each result.
2. Identical decision payloads for identical validated inputs and formulation versions; presentation/model prose may vary.
3. Complete registered structured workflows with zero model calls; measure model calls, cost, latency and fallback rate separately.
4. Show exactly what is known, stale, disputed, estimated or missing. A source citation is not proof of its accuracy.
5. Maintain independent Aegis integrity evidence, replayable calculations and accountable approvals.
6. Support desktop nested-card work and mobile chat/card navigation without losing context.
7. Improve registered rules using measured outcomes, evaluation sets and controlled promotion.

## Initial users and scenario

NGO coordinators and analysts reviewing an incident, source freshness, resource needs and available supplies. The first scenario is a demonstration using public hazard metadata and explicitly synthetic resource inputs.
Example: demand 120 units, available 85 units, reserve 5 units -> usable 80, shortfall 40, with unit/region/as-of checks and evidence references. This does not determine a safe physical delivery route.

## MVP boundaries

Include incident intake, evidence cards, resource arithmetic, workflow status, optional language parsing, provider backup, audit export and a reproducible demo.
Defer autonomous dispatch, payment execution, beneficiary personal records, claims of authoritative shelter capacities, optimization of real rescue routes and market trading.
Financial histories are potential context for procurement/exchange-cost analysis, not disaster severity evidence.

## Cost and truth claims

Aim for low operating cost and eventual affordable access. “Free to NGO users” is a business decision; cloud/model/map services are not guaranteed zero cost.
Do not promise zero hallucinations or describe all outputs as deterministic. Determinism applies to specified code contracts and pinned inputs; model output is untrusted until checked.
The reported Storyboard cost is a learning example, not an independently audited Aegis cost forecast.
